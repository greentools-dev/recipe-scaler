    Kitchen Math Guide & Frequently Asked Questions

    Why do normal calculators fail at recipes?

    Standard decimal calculators turn 1/3 of a cup into "0.333333 cups". Nobody has a 0.33 measuring cup in their kitchen. Our deterministic algorithm snaps scaled values to standard culinary fraction standards (⅛, ¼, ⅓, ½, ⅔, ¾) so you can cook smoothly.

    What fraction formats are recognized?

    The parser detects vulgar Unicode fractions (like ½, ¼, ¾, ⅓, ⅔, ⅛), typed slash fractions (like 1/2, 3/4, 2/3), mixed numbers (like 1 1/2, 2 1/4), and range measurements (such as 2-3 cloves or 1 to 2 cups).

    Does it support metric units?

    Yes. Grams (g), kilograms (kg), milliliters (ml), and liters (l) scale proportionally alongside imperial measurements like ounces (oz), pounds (lbs), teaspoons (tsp), and tablespoons (tbsp).

    Are my recipes uploaded or saved?

    Never. The parsing engine and fraction algebra operate completely inside your local browser memory. Zero ingredients, text blocks, or session logs leave your device.
